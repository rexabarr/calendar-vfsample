FAQ

Good questions

Can you help with a cake or decorations?

Yes. Tell us what you have in mind and our team will arrange it. Extras like decor or a photographer are priced separately.

Can people arrive on different days?

Usually, yes. Your driver handles each airport pickup. Let our team know when you ask for your quote.

Can we keep it a surprise?

Yes. Tell us when you reach out, and our team will plan everything with you only.

Can everyone pay their own share?

Ask our team. We'll walk you through how to handle payment across your group when we send your quote.

Is this legit? How do I pay?

You talk with a real person on our team, and get your exact quote in writing, before you pay anything. To reserve, you pay a 50% deposit by card through our secure checkout and sign our terms. The balance is due 30 days before you arrive.

What's not included?

Flights, excursions, drinks and meals out (including your free night), gratuities and travel insurance. We recommend every guest buys their own travel insurance.

Who is my concierge?

Your personal concierge handles your trip from booking to departure. Our AI concierge assistant is available 24/7 for quick answers, and anything it can't handle goes straight to your personal concierge and our team in the DR.

What if we have to cancel?

Cancel 30 or more days before arrival and you get a full refund, minus a 5% booking fee. Closer to your trip, partial refunds apply under our published tiers, and we walk you through them before you pay anything. Date changes are possible for a rebooking fee, subject to availability.

How far is the beach?

Our villas are minutes from the beach. On beach days your driver can bring all of your beach supplies, coolers included, and help you set up.

What about getting around safely?

Your driver takes you to and from scheduled outings, security comes along, and your concierge always knows your plans.
