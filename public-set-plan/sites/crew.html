FAQ

Good questions

Can we add people later?

Usually, yes. Bigger groups move into a bigger villa and the per-person price drops. Tell our team and we'll requote.

What about nightlife?

Your concierge can book lounges, rooftops and VIP tables, and your driver takes you there and brings you back. Drinks and table minimums are paid separately.

Is this legit? How do I pay?

You'll talk with a real person on our team before you pay anything. To reserve, you pay a 50% deposit by card through our secure checkout and sign our terms. The balance is due 30 days before you arrive.

What's not included?

Flights, excursions, drinks and meals out (including your free night), gratuities and travel insurance. We recommend every guest buys their own travel insurance.

Who is my concierge?

Your personal concierge handles your trip from booking to departure. Our AI concierge assistant is available 24/7 for quick answers, and anything it can't handle goes straight to your personal concierge and our team in the DR.

What if someone in the group cancels?

Cancellations follow our published tiers, based on how many days before arrival you cancel. We'll walk you through them before you book. Date changes are possible for a rebooking fee, subject to availability.

How far is the beach?

Our villas are minutes from the beach, and your driver can take you there on scheduled outings.

What about getting around safely?

Your driver takes you to and from scheduled outings, security comes along, and your concierge always knows your plans. Your welcome kit includes local tips and emergency contacts.
