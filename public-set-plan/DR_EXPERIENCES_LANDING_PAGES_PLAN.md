# DR Experiences – Three Landing Page Plans

**Purpose:** a page-by-page plan for three audience-specific landing pages. Each page shows teaser prices and a contact form that sends the lead to the sales team. **Nothing here is built yet.** This is the plan a designer, a VA, or Claude Code builds from.
**Written:** Nov 17, 2026 · **Sources:** `Villa_Getaway_Expenses_v10.xlsx` (updated Public Set + Price tab), `DR_Experiences_explanation_written_story.docx`, `PUBLIC_SET_GAME_PLAN.md`

## 1. The three pages at a glance

| | Page 1 – **The Crew** | Page 2 – **The Getaway** | Page 3 – **The Celebration** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Who** | Guys trips: the group chat that never books, friends reconnecting, guys who want the nightlife and social scene | Girls' weekends and moms who need a break | Milestones: 40th/50th birthdays, divorce / "new chapter" parties, reunions, mixed friend groups |
| **Core feeling** | "We finally did it." Freedom, nights out, zero planning | "Someone else handles everything." Rest, fun, being taken care of | "This one deserves to be big." A once-a-decade moment |
| **Suggested URL** | `[DOMAIN]/crew` | `[DOMAIN]/getaway` | `[DOMAIN]/celebrate` |
| **Lead group size** | 4–8 | 4 (moms often 3–4) | 6–8 |
| **Teaser price** | From **$1,320/person** | From **$1,510/person** | From **$1,320/person** |
| **Main traffic** | IG Reels, organizer referrals, warm network | FB (groups + ads), IG Stories | IG, FB, word of mouth, email |

**Why three pages on one domain:** one brand (DR Experiences) and one domain build trust, reviews and search ranking in one place. Each audience still lands on a page that speaks only to them, and each ad or post links to its own page. The **hostess brand stays fully separate** (no links, shared pixels or shared accounts).

**Why there's no separate "singles / meet people abroad" page:** those visitors are served on **The Crew** (and The Getaway) through nightlife and the social scene. A page aimed at "meeting people" would be treated by Meta as a dating service or solicitation and could get the ad account banned (see the guardrails in section 7).

## 3. Shared building blocks (all three pages)

### 3a. Page skeleton (same order on every page, with different copy and photos)
1. **Top bar:** logo, one link ("How it works"), button "Get my group's price"
2. **Hero:** full-width photo or video, headline, subhead, teaser price, CTA
3. **The story:** an audience-specific version of your "feet in the pool" story
4. **What's included:** 6 icons + short lines
5. **How it works:** 3 steps
6. **The team:** chef, concierges, driver, security, housekeeper (photo cards)
7. **A day in the life:** an itinerary strip (morning, afternoon, night)
8. **Excursions:** a photo grid with "at least 15% below resort prices"
9. **Villa vs. resort:** a short comparison
10. **Teaser price block**
11. **Reviews:** placeholder until real reviews exist (never invent them)
12. **FAQ:** 6–8 questions
13. **Contact form** (section 3c)
14. **Footer:** terms, privacy, guest conduct policy, business address, email/phone, social links

### 3b. "What's included" (from your story; see section 8 for items to confirm)
- 🏝 **Private villa with a private pool**, about 10 minutes from the beach
- 🍽 **Chef-prepared meals:** breakfast and lunch by the housekeeper/cook, dinners by the private chef
- 🚙 **Private car service** with security for scheduled outings
- 🗣 **Two English-speaking concierges**, a text away
- 🌴 **Excursions at least 15% below resort prices** (booked separately)
- 🎁 Welcome kit on arrival

**Not included (always shown):** flights, excursions, drinks and meals out, gratuities, travel insurance.

**Hidden fields:** page (crew/getaway/celebrate), UTM source/medium/campaign/content, referrer code (`?ref=`), timestamp.

**After submit:**
- Thank-you message on the page: *"Got it! A member of our team will reach out within [X business hours] by [chosen method]. Want a head start? Check out a sample itinerary →"*
- Auto-email confirmation to the guest
- Instant alert to the sales team (email + text) and a new CRM record tagged with the page and occasion
- **Response promise: [OWNER: within 1 business hour, 9am–9pm ET?].** Speed wins group trips.
- Spam protection: a honeypot field + reCAPTCHA/Turnstile. No phone verification (it adds friction).

### 3d. Visual direction (shared)
- **Real photos first.** The hero and villa sections are always real photos of the actual villa once the DR shoot is done.
- **Palette:** warm sand, ocean teal, deep green, and white. Each page gets one accent: Crew = deep navy/charcoal, Getaway = coral/blush, Celebration = gold.
- **Type:** a bold serif for headlines, a clean sans for body text.
- **Mobile first:** most traffic is from IG/FB on phones. The form must be finishable with one thumb in under 60 seconds.

## 7. Guardrails (all pages, all ads)
- **No sexual or dating implication.** Banned words and phrases: "meet women/men," "ladies," "companion," "dating," "lonely," "passport bro," "anything goes," "what happens in DR," and suggestive emojis.
- **Write about the trip, not the person.** Avoid "Divorced?", "Single?", "Over 40?" in ads.
- **Claims:** no "guaranteed," "unforgettable," "safe," "licensed," "insured" until documented. Describe staff by role.
- **Your story doc:** see section 8 for lines to change before publishing.
- **Images:** created images (AI or staged) must never be presented as the actual villa or the actual staff (see section 9).
- **Reviews:** real only, with written permission. Leave the section as an honest placeholder until then.

## 10. Tracking and hand-off to sales
- **UTMs** on every ad, post and email link: `utm_source` (ig/fb/email/referral), `utm_campaign` (crew/getaway/celebrate), `utm_content` (creative ID).
- **Pixel/conversion events:** Page view → Form start → Form submit (Meta Pixel + Conversions API, GA4).
- **CRM pipeline:** New lead → Contacted → Call held → Quote sent → Deposit → Traveled → Review → Referral. Each lead is tagged with the page and occasion.
- **Weekly per page:** visits, form-start rate, submit rate (target 3–6% of visits), lead→call rate, call→deposit rate, cost per lead.
- **A/B tests after 200+ visits per page:** headline, hero image (real video vs. photo), and showing vs. hiding the teaser price in the hero.
