- Six trips means **6 organizers saying yes**, which is roughly 24 guests a month. Every asset is built to win over **the organizer** and to give them what they need to sell the trip in their own group chat.
- Capacity check: 6 trips/month uses about 70% of the current 2-groups-per-weekend limit. Capacity isn't the bottleneck; demand is.
- **Booking lead time:** groups usually book 4–10 weeks ahead. To run 6 trips in a month, about 6 deposits need to land **1–2 months before it.** All targets below are tracked by **deposits taken**, not trips run.

### ⚠️ Guardrails for the "meet people / singles / lonely guys" angle (read before writing any copy)
This segment can be served, but **how it's marketed is the biggest risk to the business.** The DR is under scrutiny for sex tourism. Meta automatically removes ads and accounts that hint at arranged romantic or sexual contact, and U.S. federal law (the PROTECT Act) and DR law are enforced hard against exploitation.
- **Sell the scene, not the people.** Nightlife, beach clubs, a driver to take you out and get you home safely, meeting other travelers. **Never** imply that the trip, the staff or the brand arranges introductions, dates or companionship.
- **Banned words in all copy, ads and DMs:** "meet women/men," "ladies," "companion," "dating," "lonely," "passport bro," "no luck in the US," "anything goes," "what happens in DR," and sexualized emojis such as 🍑🍆💦😈.
- **Meta personal-attribute rule:** ads can't say or imply things about the viewer ("Divorced?", "Single and lonely?", "Over 40?"). Write about the **trip**, not the person: say "Divorce party villa," not "Recently divorced?"
- **Meta dating policy:** anything that reads as a dating service needs Meta's prior written approval. Stay clear of it.
- **Guest conduct policy** in the terms and booking flow: zero tolerance for exploitation, anything involving minors, illegal drugs, or bringing unvetted visitors into the villa. Staff are briefed and security has authority to end a stay. This protects your staff, your villa agreement and the brand.
- Public Set is **hostess-free**, which makes ads and pricing posts much safer than the hostess brand. Keep it that way, and never cross-promote the two brands.

## 5. Channel strategy

### 5a. Warm network first (months 1–3: expected to bring about 70% of early bookings)
Your network (35–55, about $175K) is the fastest way to the first 6 groups.
1. Build a **list of 150 names** (VA formats it into the CRM): friends, coworkers, clients, church/alumni/fraternity/gym/barbershop contacts. Tag each one with a likely segment (guys, girls, moms, birthday).
2. Send **personal 1:1 texts and DMs** (not group blasts) using the scripts in B5, about 20 a day. The ask isn't "book a trip." It's "**who in your circle is always trying to plan a trip?**" plus the founding offer.
3. Find **5 "spark organizers"**: people who always plan the friend-group trips. Give them the organizer kit (B7).
4. Every founding group is a **content shoot and review machine**. Their photos and testimonials fuel months 3–6.

### 5b. Instagram (main content channel)
- **Handle:** `[OWNER]` brand name + "villas" or "trips", the same on IG, FB, TikTok (reserve it), domain and email.
- **Profile:** Link-in-bio leads to the landing page (B2). Highlights: *Villa · Chef · Excursions · Pricing · Reviews · FAQ · How to Book.*
- **Cadence:** 4 Reels/week, 2 carousels/week, daily Stories (polls, Q&A, behind the scenes). The DR team shoots in batches using the shot list (B9).
- **Content pillars:**
  1. **The villa**: walk-throughs, pool at golden hour, bedroom tours ("who gets the master?")
  2. **The food**: chef plating, breakfast spread, "what our chef made tonight"
  3. **The day**: excursion clips, catamaran, golf, ATVs, "a day in the Public Set"
  4. **The math**: "What $1,225/person gets you vs. a resort" carousel, with the price shown openly
  5. **The group**: friend-group reactions, birthday surprises, arrival-day reveals (once there are guests)
  6. **Trust**: reviews, "meet your driver/chef," how deposits and cancellations work, FAQ
- **Engagement routine (VA, 30 min/day):** reply to every comment and DM within 2 hours during U.S. daytime; engage with 30 accounts a day in PHL/DC/ATL/NJ (local restaurants, birthday-party planners, local influencers, rooftop bars).
- **DM keyword funnel:** posts end with "Comment **VILLA** and we'll send the price breakdown." Use ManyChat (or similar) to auto-DM the landing page and the planner lead magnet.

### 5c. Facebook
- **Business Page** mirrors IG (cross-post Reels).
- **Groups:** join local PHL, DC, ATL and NJ groups for moms, birthday planning, travel deals, Black travel groups (strong East Coast DR market), and alumni groups. **Add value first** (DR tips, "how we split a group trip" guides) and follow each group's promotion rules.
- **FB Events / Lives:** a monthly "Virtual Villa Tour + Q&A" live with the DR team walking the villa.
- **FB is the main ad platform for moms' and girls' trips**, since those audiences skew FB in the 35–55 range.

### 5d. Email (the conversion engine, so every channel feeds it)
- **Lead magnet:** "*The Group Trip Planner: Dominican Republic Edition*": a PDF/page with a sample itinerary, a per-person price breakdown, a resort-vs-villa comparison, and a "how to get your group to commit" script. Opt-in on the landing page and via the IG keyword DM.
- **Sequences** (copy in B4):
  1. Welcome/nurture (7 emails over 14 days) for new leads
  2. Organizer follow-up after a quote or call (4 emails)
  3. Booked-guest onboarding (pre-trip countdown, packing list, excursion menu)
  4. Post-trip (review request, photo delivery, referral ask, rebook offer)
  5. Monthly newsletter (new dates, open weekends, guest stories)
- **Compliance:** CAN-SPAM requires a physical mailing address, a working unsubscribe link and honest subject lines. SMS needs express written opt-in (TCPA). Canada (CASL) is out of scope for now.

### 5e. Paid ads (start in month 3, once real content and 2+ reviews exist)
- **Platforms:** Meta (IG + FB) only to start.
- **Geo:** 25–40 mile radius around Philadelphia, Washington DC, Atlanta, and all of New Jersey. Lean on Newark (EWR) and PHL catchments, since both have nonstop flights to Punta Cana. `[Verify current nonstops from EWR/PHL/IAD/BWI/ATL before quoting them in ads.]`
- **Structure:** (1) video views on the best Reels; (2) retargeting of video viewers, IG engagers and site visitors with the lead magnet; (3) retargeting of leads with "open weekends" and testimonials.
- **Budget `[OWNER]`:** start at **$30–50/day** (about $900–1,500/month). The workbook's $100/booking marketing line can't support paid ads at founding prices. At full price, you can afford roughly **$400–700 to acquire a booking** and still keep about $1,900+ profit.
- **Kill rule:** pause any ad set that spends 2× the target cost per lead without leads. Target cost per lead: **$8–20** (to be confirmed from real data).

### 5f. Referrals and partners (month 4+)
- **Organizer reward:** the organizer of a full-price group of 4 gets **$250 off** (or a free excursion). Past guests who refer a booked group get **$200 credit** toward a future trip. FTC rule: referrers who post must disclose (#ad or "I get a credit").
- **Partners:** barbershops and salons (moms, girls trips), birthday/event planners, alumni chapters, golf clubs (guys trips), and local micro-creators in PHL/DC/ATL (free founding spot in exchange for disclosed content).

## 6. Sales process: lead to deposit

```
IG/FB/Email/Referral → Landing page or "VILLA" DM
   → Lead magnet opt-in (email) + 3-question quiz (group size, dates, occasion)
   → 15-min "Plan Your Trip" call (Calendly) — owner or VA
   → Quote sent within 24 hrs (quote tool, B6) + group-share page
   → Organizer shares with group chat → 50% deposit (Stripe) + signed terms
   → Onboarding emails → split-pay balance at 30 days → trip → review + referral
```

**Speed rule:** reply to every inquiry within 1 hour during business hours, and always within the same day. Most group trips go to whoever answers first.

## 9. Business plan essentials

- **Model:** experience operator. Pieces (villa, chef, driver, housekeeper, security) are bought per booking, so there are no fixed staff costs. The spread is the profit.
- **Unit economics:** founding about 8% margin (customer-acquisition investment), early about 25%, full about 36% (workbook).
- **Startup costs to plan `[OWNER to fill]`:** LLC + attorney consult, domain + site, email platform, CRM, ManyChat, Canva Pro, VA, ad budget, the content shoot, the pilot trip subsidy, and a **cash reserve that covers at least 1 vendor refund/emergency per month**.
- **Risks and mitigations:**
  - *New-brand trust at $1K+/person:* real photos, video tours, live "Meet the Team" calls, reviews, clear terms, and deposits through Stripe with a business name on the receipt.
  - *Vendor no-show:* a backup driver and chef on file; the DR contact on call for every trip.
  - *Weather/hurricanes:* a written date-change policy; advise guests to buy travel insurance.
  - *Meta account loss:* a separate brand, the guardrails in section 4, and an email list plus website as the owned backup.
  - *Thin founding margin:* lock vendor quotes in writing before taking deposits.
- **Legal (questions for the attorney, not legal advice):** seller-of-travel registration based on where customers live (check PA, DC, GA and NJ, plus CA/FL/HI/WA if they sell there); customer terms, guest conduct policy and waiver; contractor agreements; content-ownership/permission release for guest content.
